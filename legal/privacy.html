CleanBid policies

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026  ·  Questions? getfinova@gmail.com

CleanBid is estimating software for commercial cleaning businesses. It runs in your web browser. Depending on how you use it, your data either stays in your browser's local storage on your own computer, or is stored in a private cloud workspace that only you and the people you invite can access. CleanBid does not sell your information, does not run advertising, and does not use third-party analytics or tracking.

Scope of this policy

This policy covers the CleanBid web application (the quote builder at this site) and its legal pages. It applies to the two ways CleanBid runs:

  • Local / demo mode. The default. No account needed — click “Try Demo” or start using the app without signing in.
  • Signed-in workspace mode (cloud). Available when email sign-in / account creation is enabled. Your quotes, customers, properties, pricing profiles, and workspace settings are saved to a private database for your workspace.

Information users enter into CleanBid

CleanBid works with the business information you type into it. Depending on which features you use, that can include:

  • Your business profile: company name, phone number, email address, website, address, tagline, license or trust line, and branding color used on proposals.
  • Customer records: customer companies, contact names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, and notes.
  • Properties: building names, addresses, square footage, floors, and property types.
  • Quotes and proposals: areas, measurements, tasks, frequencies, package choices, add-ons, labor rates, burden, overhead, supplies percentages, target margins, calculated prices, price overrides, revision history, proposal text, payment terms, and follow-up dates.
  • Pricing profiles and settings: wage, burden, overhead, margin, minimum price, currency, productivity rates per area type, task lists, contract terms, and default proposal terms text.
  • Account information (signed-in mode only): name, email address, password (handled by our authentication provider), company name, and workspace name.
  • Import files: if you import customers from CSV/TSV or Excel files, CleanBid reads those files in your browser to fill in customer and property records. Imported rows are handled like any other data you enter.

You are responsible for having permission to enter information about your customers and their buildings into CleanBid.

Information collected automatically

CleanBid itself collects almost nothing automatically. The app sets no analytics identifiers, runs no tracking scripts, and keeps no browsing history.

  • In local/demo mode: nothing is sent anywhere by the app. There is no automatic collection to describe.
  • In signed-in mode: authentication requests necessarily transmit your email address and session tokens so you stay signed in. Your workspace's activity trail may record actions such as quote creation, for your own reference.
  • Server logs: like any website, this site is served over the internet. If the site is hosted by a hosting provider or behind its network, that provider's standard logs may record IP addresses and request details for security and operations. See “Third-party services” below.

How information is used

Information in CleanBid is used only to make the product work:

  • To calculate estimates, prices, margins, and proposals from your inputs;
  • To save and restore your work between sessions (browser storage or your private workspace database);
  • To render branded proposals you can print or export;
  • To let you export backups and re-import them later;
  • In signed-in mode, to authenticate you, associate your data with your workspace, and enforce who can access which workspace;
  • To show in-app messages when something needs your attention (validation warnings, save errors).

CleanBid does not use your information to profile you, advertise to anyone, train machine-learning models, or build analytics audiences.

Browser local storage

In local and demo mode, CleanBid keeps everything in your browser's localStorage. This is storage that lives on your device, tied to this website, readable only by this site in that browser. It is not a cookie and it is never sent to any server. Keys used include:

  • cleanbid_v3 (plus optional per-workspace suffixes) — your workspace data in signed-out local mode;
  • cleanbid_demo_v3 — the separate demo dataset;
  • cleanbid_active_workspace — remembers which workspace was last open;
  • cleanbid_safety_snapshots_v1 — automatic safety snapshots taken before restores;
  • cleanbid_last_export_* — the timestamp of your last backup export.

Because local data lives only in your browser, clearing your browser data for this site deletes it. Keep exports of anything important (see Data deletion).

Quote and proposal data

Quotes are the core record in CleanBid: measurements, tasks, rates, calculated prices, any manual price overrides, revision history, and proposal text. In local/demo mode they exist only in your browser. In signed-in mode they are saved to your workspace in the cloud database so they follow you between devices and can be shared with teammates in the same workspace. Quote numbers and revision history are generated locally by the app.

Proposals are generated in your browser from quote data and printed or saved as PDF using your browser's print dialog. CleanBid does not email proposals for you and does not send quotes to anyone.

Customer and business information

The customer and property records you keep in CleanBid are usually personal data about your clients — their companies, contacts, phone numbers, and building addresses. Whoever operates this CleanBid installation decides how long those records are kept and how requests from your customers about their data are handled. If you run a cleaning business using CleanBid, you are generally the controller of that data and should respond to your customers' privacy requests yourself; we provide export tools so you can retrieve or review what you hold.

Does CleanBid send my information to a server?

Short answer: not unless you sign in.
  • Signed out (local/demo): all pricing math runs in your browser, and all data stays in your browser. No quote, customer, or business information leaves your device.
  • Signed in (cloud): the app saves your workspace data — customers, properties, quotes, profiles, settings — to the CleanBid database so it syncs across your devices and is shared with your workspace members. Access is restricted server-side to members of your workspace.

Third-party services

CleanBid deliberately uses very few outside services:

  • Supabase — when sign-in is enabled, Supabase provides authentication and hosts the PostgreSQL database used for workspace data (Supabase Inc., with data hosting in the project's configured region). Supabase processes account credentials and your workspace data as our infrastructure provider. Their own privacy practices are described at supabase.com/privacy.
  • Vercel, Inc. — this site is deployed on Vercel, which serves the application files. Like any host, Vercel processes request logs for security and operational purposes. See vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy.
  • Google Fonts — the app loads two typefaces (Fraunces and Inter) from Google Fonts. Requests to Google Fonts expose your IP address to Google as with any web resource. See Google's privacy policy.
  • SheetJS CDN — only if you import an .xlsx/Excel file, the app downloads the SheetJS reader library from cdn.sheetjs.com at that moment. This request happens only when an Excel import is attempted; CSV imports need no external library.

CleanBid does not use advertising networks, social media pixels, analytics services (e.g., Google Analytics), session replay, AI processing services, or marketing automation. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Analytics and tracking

None. There is no first-party or third-party analytics, telemetry, fingerprinting, or tracking anywhere in the application or on these policy pages.

Cookies

CleanBid does not set cookies for its own purposes. When you sign in, our authentication provider (Supabase) maintains your login session using browser storage set under its own domain configuration — see the Cookie Policy for the full picture.

Data retention

  • Local/demo data stays in your browser until you delete it there (clearing site data) — indefinitely otherwise.
  • Workspace data is kept while your workspace exists. Deleting records removes them from the active database.
  • Account records: deleting your user account removes it from the authentication provider; ask us via getfinova@gmail.com. Backups created by you remain wherever you saved them.
  • Hosting logs are retained only per the hosting provider's default short retention window.

Data deletion

You can delete your data at any time:

  • Local/demo: clear this site's data in your browser settings, or use the app's reset controls. This permanently erases everything stored in that browser.
  • Individual records: delete customers, properties, or quotes inside the app yourself.
  • Your whole workspace / account: contact getfinova@gmail.com and we will remove the workspace and associated account records.

Note that once data is deleted it cannot be recovered unless you exported a backup beforehand.

Security

Measures in place include: encrypted transport (HTTPS), authentication handled by the auth provider rather than the app itself, and Row Level Security rules on the database that restrict every row to members of its workspace — one workspace cannot read another's data. Local-mode data has no protection beyond your own device and browser profile, so use disk encryption and lock your machine. No system is perfectly secure; please don't store sensitive categories of data (health, government IDs, payment card numbers) in CleanBid — there is no reason to, since none of the fields require them.

Children's privacy

CleanBid is a business tool aimed at cleaning-company owners and estimators. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through CleanBid, contact getfinova@gmail.com and we will delete it.

International users

CleanBid is operated from an undisclosed location; the hosting provider, database provider, and font CDN may process data in the United States and other countries. If you use CleanBid from outside the United States, your information may be transferred to and processed in those locations. We do not claim certification under any specific international transfer framework.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights such as accessing your data, correcting it, deleting it, or objecting to certain processing. Because CleanBid is built to put your data in your hands already, many of these rights you can exercise directly:

  • Access & portability: export a full JSON backup from the app at any time, or copy data out of your browser storage.
  • Correction & deletion: edit or delete records in the app directly.
  • Withdrawal of consent / account deletion: stop using signed-in mode and contact getfinova@gmail.com.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not process personal information for targeted advertising, so related opt-outs (such as CCPA/CPA "do not sell or share") are satisfied by design. If you cannot exercise a right yourself through the app, contact us at getfinova@gmail.com. Where laws like the GDPR apply, the legal bases for processing are: performing our contract with you (providing the service), legitimate interests (securing and operating the service), and consent where specifically required.

Changes to this policy

If CleanBid's data practices change (for example, adding a newsletter or analytics), we will update this page and change the "Last updated" date above. Material changes affecting signed-in users will be communicated through the app or by email where we have an address for you.
