Description of the service

CleanBid is a web application that helps commercial cleaning businesses turn site-survey information into calculated estimates, priced quotes, and client-ready proposals. It provides structured input forms (areas, tasks, frequencies), a pricing engine that computes labor, burden, supplies, overhead, add-ons and margins from your own rates and assumptions, saved customer and property records, quote revision history, branded printable proposals, and CSV/Excel import plus JSON backup export.

CleanBid can run in two modes: a signed-out local/demo mode in which all data stays in your browser, and an authenticated workspace mode in which data is stored server-side. Features may vary between these modes.

Accuracy of quote and proposal information

CleanBid calculates exactly what its formulas say from the numbers you provide. It cannot know whether a building's square footage is right, whether a crew can actually clean it in the estimated hours, or whether your wage, burden, overhead, margin, or productivity assumptions match reality. You are responsible for verifying survey measurements, labor assumptions, material costs, and the final price before presenting a proposal. A proposal generated by CleanBid is your document, based on your inputs.

Pricing calculations

The pricing engine applies fixed arithmetic — crew hours from area sizes and task times, headcount, wage, burden, supplies, overhead, package multipliers, add-ons, target-margin clamps, and minimum-price floors — to the assumptions you configure. Results are estimates of cost and price under those assumptions. They are not appraisals, market advice, or guarantees of profitability, and rounding behavior (for example, monthly price floors) is part of the tool's design.
