Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026  ·  Questions? getfinova@gmail.com

This policy covers what you may and may not do with CleanBid. It is part of the Terms of Use. It applies to every mode of the service — the public demo, signed-out local use, and cloud workspaces alike.

Use CleanBid lawfully

  • No unlawful activity of any kind through the service.
  • No fraud or misrepresentation — including fake quotes, fabricated credentials, or deceptive pricing presented to your customers.
  • No use of CleanBid to operate an illegal business, launder money, or evade sanctions.
  • No infringement of anyone's intellectual-property rights (for example, pasting copyrighted text into proposal templates without rights to it).

Don't attack or abuse the service

  • No attempts to compromise the application — injecting malicious code into inputs or imports (the app escapes hostile strings in normal rendering, but don't try), probing for vulnerabilities without permission, or denial-of-service.
  • No automated scraping, bulk harvesting, load testing, or hammering of the site or its APIs without written permission.
  • No interference with other users' access, workspaces, or data.

Respect access boundaries

  • No unauthorized access: only use workspaces you belong to, with accounts you're entitled to.
  • No sharing one account among people or companies that aren't members of that workspace.
  • No circumventing authentication, Row-Level Security rules, rate limits, or other technical protections.
  • The demo is for evaluating CleanBid — please don't treat it as free storage or a sandbox for attacking the platform.

Handle customer information properly

  • Only enter customers, contacts, and properties you have permission to store and process.
  • No misuse of customer information: no selling contact data, no spamming people whose details are in your records, no repurposing another company's customer lists.
  • No special-category data (health, biometrics, government IDs, payment card numbers) — CleanBid has no fields that need them and they don't belong here.

Files you import

CleanBid accepts CSV/TSV/Excel imports of customer and property lists, and JSON backup restores, processed locally in your browser. Don't import files containing malware or unlawful content, and don't attempt to smuggle executable code through file contents. Restores must be genuine CleanBid exports.

What happens if this policy is violated

We may warn, suspend, or terminate access, remove content, and — where warranted — report unlawful activity to the appropriate authorities. If your violation damages us or others, the indemnification provisions of the Terms of Use apply.

Reporting problems

Found a security issue or someone abusing CleanBid? Tell us at getfinova@gmail.com. Responsible disclosure is welcome; we ask for reasonable time to fix issues before public disclosure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as features change; the "Last updated" date above will move. Continued use after changes means acceptance.
