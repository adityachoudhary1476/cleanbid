# CleanBid Pilot Outreach Emails

Personalize the [bracketed] bits. Keep subject lines lowercase-ish and human —
they get more replies than "EXCITING OPPORTUNITY!!!" style.

---

## EMAIL 1 — The main pilot pitch (use for most companies)

**Subject:** free tool for your bidding headaches? (pilot invite)

Hi [First name / Owner name],

I'll keep this short — I've built a web app called CleanBid that does one thing:
takes commercial cleaning quotes from hours to minutes.

Walk a property, punch in areas + square footage, pick frequency, and it gives
you a live monthly/annual price with your own wage, burden, overhead and margin
numbers baked in — plus a branded proposal you can print or send on the spot.

I'm looking for 3–5 owner-operated cleaning companies to use it free during the
pilot in exchange for straight feedback on what's missing.

If quoting/bidding eats too much of your week, want 5 minutes of screenshots?
No strings, no card, no contract — just tell me what breaks.

[Your name]
[phone] · [link to cleanbid site]

P.S. happy to do a quick 15-min screen-share where we price one of YOUR real
buildings live. You keep the numbers either way.

## Sending tips

- Send from your normal address, plain text, no images/logos (spam filters).
- One personalized line per company beats any template polish.
- 5 emails/day max from a fresh sender address.
- Track who replied in a simple sheet: Company | Sent | Replied | Status.
