## Where to find more (when you want to scale past 20)

- **BSCAI member directory:** apps.bscai.org/publicDirectory — 1,000+ contractor members,
  filter US/Canada by state. Members are exactly the "serious about bidding" segment.
- **IJCSA directory:** ijcsa.org — smaller/janitorial-scale owners, cheaper members,
  likely more price-sensitive but hungrier.
- **JanSource Texas directory:** jansource.com — per-state provider listings with contacts.
- **inven.ai lists:** top-19/20/23/25 company roundups for OH/MI/TX used above.

## Sequencing advice

- Send 5 first (pick from Tier 1 + your favorites in Tier 2), wait 48h, then the rest.
- Personalize ONE line per email (the "why you" column above) — that line is what gets replies.
- Tuesday–Thursday mornings send best. Follow up once after 4 business days.
